A LITTLE HELP IN THE KITCHEN

帮助与联系

遇到问题，或有一个让 Mise 更好用的建议，都可以联系我们。

邮件：zyfeleven@gmail.com
公开功能建议：GitHub 反馈页。请勿公开邀请码、密钥、购买凭证或私人记录。

反馈时带上这些信息

App 版本、手机型号、系统版本、操作步骤及错误提示会帮助我们定位问题。截图请遮住私人信息。不要发送密码或完整邀请码；若需要进一步核验，我们会通过邮件说明。

Agent 为什么停下来了？

任务需要你保持 App 在前台。它可能在等待你的回答、确认、预算调整或网络恢复。查看对话中的提示后继续；不要反复新建相同任务。已完成的操作可在记录中查看，支持的改动可撤销；有后续修改冲突时会说明原因。

AI 不能使用怎么办？

检查邀请码、网络和点数。提供 AI 数据确认界面的版本还需先同意相关处理；可在设置的“隐私与支持”查看或撤回。当前没有公开充值入口，额度或邀请码问题可联系维护者，手动功能仍可使用。

如何保留数据？

安装更新时覆盖已有 App，不要先卸载。库存、菜谱和做饭记录主要保存在本机，目前没有账号或云端厨房备份。撤销厨房操作不会退回已经消耗的 AI 点数。

隐私和删除请求

阅读隐私政策了解处理范围。数据访问、更正和删除请求请发邮件，不要在公开 Issue 中发送证明材料。

ENGLISH

Help & contact

Email zyfeleven@gmail.com for support or privacy requests. Share public feature suggestions on GitHub. Never post invite codes, keys, purchase receipts or private kitchen records publicly.

Reporting a problem

Include your app version, device and OS version, steps and error message. Redact private information in screenshots. Do not send passwords or full invite codes; we will explain any necessary verification by email.

Agent tasks

Keep Mise in the foreground while a task runs. It may pause for clarification, confirmation, budget or network recovery. Follow the conversation’s instructions instead of repeatedly starting duplicate tasks. Inspect completed actions and undo supported changes; later edits may prevent a safe undo.

AI access and your data

Check your invite, connection and credits. Versions with AI permission controls also require your agreement before sending AI content; review or withdraw it under Settings → Privacy & support. Public top-ups are not available yet. Contact the maintainer about access or credit issues; manual tools remain available.

Install updates over the existing app. Do not uninstall first: kitchen records are primarily local and account/cloud kitchen backup is not available. Undo does not refund AI credits already used. See the privacy policy and email us for data access, correction or deletion requests.
