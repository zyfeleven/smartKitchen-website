PRIVACY, IN PLAIN WORDS

隐私政策

更新日期：2026 年 9 月 30 日

厨房记录主要保存在你的设备上。使用 AI 时，需要把相关内容交给服务器和模型服务处理。

Mise 食序由项目维护者提供。隐私、数据访问或删除请求请联系 zyfeleven@gmail.com。请勿在公开 GitHub Issue 中发送邀请码、密码或私人厨房记录。

我们处理哪些数据

  • 设备上的厨房记录：库存、食材批次、菜谱、购物清单、用餐安排、做饭记录、Agent 任务及操作记录主要保存在本机。Mise 当前不提供账号或跨设备厨房数据同步；系统备份可能另行保存设备数据，以你的系统设置为准。
  • AI 输入：在你使用相关功能时，文字、选择的图片、菜谱链接，以及完成任务所需的库存、候选菜谱、任务目标、对话和工具结果，会发送到 Mise 的香港服务器。AI 所需内容继续发送给 DeepSeek，用于识别、生成菜谱和推进厨房任务。不会因为打开相册就上传整个照片库。
  • 服务记录：服务器保留邀请码验证和管理记录、AI 请求标识、时间、模型、Token 用量、点数变动及技术错误信息。网络服务可能产生包含 IP 地址等连接信息的访问日志。我们用这些记录提供服务、避免重复扣点、排查问题和防止滥用。
  • 支持信息：你通过邮件主动提交的联系信息和问题内容，用于回复、排障或处理数据请求。

可选的助手记忆

支持记忆功能的新版 App 默认关闭助手记忆。开启后，助手可查阅相关本机历史，并把少量已确认偏好及相关历史片段用于后续 AI 请求，发送至上述服务器和模型服务。推测的偏好会先供你确认；你也可查看来源、编辑或忘记记忆，或关闭功能以停止后续调用。记忆保存在本机，不提供账号同步。“忘记”会停用后续使用，支持的撤销和操作记录仍可能保留本机副本；关闭或忘记不能撤回已发送的数据或删除第三方记录。

第三方服务与处理地点

Mise 后端托管于阿里云香港服务器。AI 服务商为 DeepSeek，其隐私政策说明数据在中国大陆处理和存储；相关处理可能发生在你所在地区之外。DeepSeek 的数据使用和保留规则请参阅其隐私政策。我们不承诺第三方零保留或不用于训练。

语音输入通过操作系统的语音识别能力实现，需要麦克风及语音权限；是否在设备上完成取决于版本、平台和系统支持，系统服务可能处理音频。Mise 后端接收的是你提交的识别文字。网页由 GitHub Pages 托管；访问页面时 GitHub 会按其隐私声明处理连接信息。

你的选择

提供授权界面的新版 App 会在首次发送 AI 内容前说明数据去向并征求同意。你可以拒绝，继续手动管理厨房；也可在“设置 → 隐私与支持”撤回同意，停止后续 AI 请求。撤回不会撤销已经发生的传输或删除已有服务记录。如果旧版没有该入口，请更新到支持该功能的版本，或停止使用 AI 功能。

相机、照片和麦克风权限可在系统设置中调整。没有使用相关功能时，不需要授予这些权限。Mise 不在当前 App 中集成广告追踪 SDK，也不出售你的个人数据。

保留与删除

本机记录保留至你在 App 中删除、清除 App 数据或卸载；系统备份需通过相应系统管理。服务端 AI 结果通常缓存约 24 小时，用于安全重试；缓存清理不等于清除用量和点数账本。邀请码、审计、用量及支持记录会按提供服务、排障、安全和适用法律所需保留，必要的备份可能在轮换时清除。

如需访问、更正或删除我们持有的数据，请邮件联系维护者，描述请求；我们可能通过私密渠道核实请求与记录的关联。不要发送完整邀请码或密钥作为第一封邮件。我们会告知可处理的范围、保留原因及进展。Mise 无法直接删除 DeepSeek、Apple、GitHub 等第三方独立保留的数据，可另行联系对应服务商。

更新与适用范围

本政策描述当前厨房 App、AI 服务和项目网站。不包含尚未开放的付费内购或账号同步；引入这些功能时会相应更新。Mise 不面向儿童设计。政策有实质变化时会更新本页，并在需要时重新征求同意。

ENGLISH

Privacy policy

Updated September 30, 2026

Mise is provided by the project maintainer. Contact zyfeleven@gmail.com for privacy, access, correction or deletion requests. Do not post invite codes or private records in public GitHub issues.

What we process

Inventory, recipes, shopping, meal plans, cooking records and Agent task/action history are primarily stored on your device. Mise does not currently offer accounts or cloud kitchen synchronization. Operating-system backups may separately retain device data according to your settings.

When you use AI, your text, selected images, recipe links and relevant kitchen context, task goals, conversation and tool results are sent to Mise’s Hong Kong server. Content needed for AI is forwarded to DeepSeek for recognition, recipe generation and task decisions. Selecting a photo does not upload your entire photo library.

The server retains invite verification/management records, request identifiers, times, models, token usage, credit entries and technical errors for service operation, safe retries, troubleshooting and abuse prevention. Network access logs may contain connection information such as IP addresses. Information you email us is used to answer your request.

Optional Agent memory

Memory is off by default in versions that support it. When enabled, the Agent can look up relevant local history and send a bounded set of confirmed preferences and relevant history excerpts with later AI requests to the server and model provider described above. Inferred preferences are presented for confirmation. Review sources, edit or forget preferences, or turn memory off to stop future use. Memory is stored on-device with no account sync. Forgetting disables future use; supported undo and action records may retain local copies. Turning memory off or forgetting cannot recall data already sent or erase third-party records.

Providers and locations

The backend is hosted on Alibaba Cloud in Hong Kong. DeepSeek’s privacy policy describes processing and storage in mainland China, potentially outside your region. Read DeepSeek’s privacy policy for its data use and retention. We do not promise zero retention or exclusion from model training by third parties.

Voice input uses the operating system’s speech recognition, with microphone/speech permission. On-device availability depends on the build, platform and system; system services may process audio. Mise’s backend receives the recognized text you submit. This website is hosted by GitHub Pages; GitHub processes connection information under its privacy statement.

Your choices

New versions with the AI permission screen disclose data sharing before the first AI content request. Decline to keep using manual kitchen tools. Withdraw permission in Settings → Privacy & support to stop future AI requests. Withdrawal does not undo previous transfers or erase service records. If an older version lacks this control, update to a supported version or stop using AI. Manage camera, photo and microphone permissions in system settings. The current app has no advertising-tracking SDK, and we do not sell personal data.

Retention and requests

Local records remain until you delete them, clear app data or uninstall; manage system backups separately. AI results are normally cached for about 24 hours for retries. Clearing this cache does not erase usage or credit ledgers. Invite, audit, usage and support records are retained as needed for service operation, security, troubleshooting and applicable legal obligations; necessary backups may clear on rotation.

Email us to request access, correction or deletion. We may privately verify your connection to the records and explain what can be removed, any required retention and progress. Do not include full invite codes or keys in your initial email. For data independently held by DeepSeek, Apple, GitHub or other providers, contact them separately.

Scope and changes

This policy covers the current kitchen app, AI service and website. Paid purchases and account synchronization are not yet available; the policy will be updated when introduced. Mise is not designed for children. Material changes will appear here and we will seek renewed consent when needed.
