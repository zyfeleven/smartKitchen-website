PRIVACY, IN PLAIN WORDS

隐私政策

更新日期：2026 年 10 月 7 日

厨房记录主要保存在你的设备上。使用 AI 时，需要把相关内容交给服务器和模型服务处理。

Mise 食序由项目维护者提供。隐私、数据访问或删除请求请联系 zyfeleven@gmail.com。请勿在公开 GitHub Issue 中发送邀请码、密码或私人厨房记录。

我们处理哪些数据

  • 设备上的厨房记录：库存、食材批次、菜谱、购物清单、用餐安排、做饭记录、Agent 任务及操作记录主要保存在本机。Mise 当前不提供账号或跨设备厨房数据同步；系统备份可能另行保存设备数据，以你的系统设置为准。
  • AI 输入：在你使用相关功能时，文字、选择的图片、菜谱链接，以及完成任务所需的库存、候选菜谱、任务目标、对话和工具结果，会发送到 Mise 的香港服务器。AI 所需内容继续发送给 DeepSeek，用于识别、生成菜谱和推进厨房任务。不会因为打开相册就上传整个照片库。
  • 服务记录：服务器保留邀请码验证和管理记录、AI 请求标识、时间、模型、Token 用量、点数变动及技术错误信息。网络服务可能产生包含 IP 地址等连接信息的访问日志。我们用这些记录提供服务、避免重复扣点、排查问题和防止滥用。
  • 支持信息：你通过邮件主动提交的联系信息和问题内容，用于回复、排障或处理数据请求。

公开版本的免费 AI 额度

提供公开免费额度的版本会在你主动领取时，为本次安装创建随机 AI 身份，并使用 Apple App Attest 验证应用真实性。App 会向 Apple 请求验证证明，再交给 Mise 服务器校验；这不包含你的厨房内容。服务器保存身份和验证密钥的散列标识，用于关联额度账本、避免重复领取和防止滥用，不保存原始验证证明。验证过程中，App 可能在系统安全存储中暂存证明以便网络失败后重试，成功后删除这份临时证明。

领取还会使用网络地址限制短时间内的重复请求。服务器把地址转为按 UTC 日期隔离的加密散列，超过两天阈值后按小时清理；短期请求计数会在内存中过期清理。代理访问日志的保留规则另行适用。身份存在期间，一次性领取和验证散列记录会随安全、用量及点数记录保留。完成验证但未领取的散列映射超过 24 小时后会定期清理；这不是实名账号，也不支持换机余额恢复。Apple 验证不能保证重装后的唯一设备识别。没有领取额度，也可以使用本机手动厨房功能。

本机备份文件

支持备份的版本允许你主动导出厨房记录，并选择是否包含菜谱照片、对话文字摘要和助手记忆。文件在设备上生成，未经加密，不包含邀请码、API 密钥或点数余额；Mise 不会自动把备份上传至后端。保存或分享至云盘、邮件或其他应用后，由你选择的服务处理。请妥善保存并自行管理副本。导入前会展示内容摘要，确认后替换本机记录；恢复的对话仅供查看，助手记忆默认关闭。这是手动转移文件，不是账号或云端同步。

可选的助手记忆

支持记忆功能的新版 App 默认关闭助手记忆。开启后，助手可查阅相关本机历史，并把少量已确认偏好及相关历史片段用于后续 AI 请求，发送至上述服务器和模型服务。推测的偏好会先供你确认；你也可查看来源、编辑或忘记记忆，或关闭功能以停止后续调用。记忆保存在本机，不提供账号同步。“忘记”会停用后续使用，支持的撤销和操作记录仍可能保留本机副本；关闭或忘记不能撤回已发送的数据或删除第三方记录。

主动提交的内测反馈

支持该功能的新版 App 可在任务中提交反馈。打开入口或预览不会上传；你确认发送后，我们才接收反馈评价、可选说明、App 版本、任务编号、步骤状态、耗时、点数及有限的诊断记录。自动附带内容不包含对话正文、照片、食材明细、长期记忆或完整邀请码；你主动填写的说明会原样发送，请勿填写密钥等敏感信息。反馈与服务器验证后的 AI 身份（邀请码或公开额度身份）关联，用于排查问题，不用于调用 AI，也不扣 AI 点。反馈保存在香港服务器的私有目录，保留 90 天后定期清理；必要备份可能在轮换时清除。你可通过下方联系方式提出访问或删除请求。

第三方服务与处理地点

Mise 后端托管于阿里云香港服务器。AI 服务商为 DeepSeek，其隐私政策说明数据在中国大陆处理和存储；相关处理可能发生在你所在地区之外。DeepSeek 的数据使用和保留规则请参阅其隐私政策。我们不承诺第三方零保留或不用于训练。

语音输入通过操作系统的语音识别能力实现，需要麦克风及语音权限；是否在设备上完成取决于版本、平台和系统支持，系统服务可能处理音频。Mise 后端接收的是你提交的识别文字。项目网站可通过 GitHub Pages 或 ChatGPT Sites 访问。香港服务器已停止托管网站和 APK 下载。相应托管服务会处理连接信息；访问 GitHub Pages 时，GitHub 会按其隐私声明处理连接信息。

你的选择

提供授权界面的新版 App 会在首次发送 AI 内容前说明数据去向并征求同意。你可以拒绝，继续手动管理厨房；也可在“设置 → 隐私与支持”撤回同意，停止后续 AI 请求。撤回不会撤销已经发生的传输或删除已有服务记录。如果旧版没有该入口，请更新到支持该功能的版本，或停止使用 AI 功能。

相机、照片和麦克风权限可在系统设置中调整。没有使用相关功能时，不需要授予这些权限。Mise 不在当前 App 中集成广告追踪 SDK，也不出售你的个人数据。

保留与删除

支持公开额度的版本可在设置中选择“删除 AI 身份和服务记录”，确认后删除该身份在活动服务器中的验证标识、点数账本、请求及结果缓存、模型用量和主动反馈。正在处理的 AI 请求需先结束；网络或清理失败时可重试。仅保留无法关联原身份的发放数量、额度和 UTC 日期统计，删除不会恢复平台发放名额。本机凭据会替换为不含身份的已删除标记，当前安装不会自动重新领取，剩余额度无法恢复。厨房记录不会随之删除，可在本机另行管理。此操作不等于立即清除独立的系统备份、服务器备份、网络日志、客服邮件或第三方服务记录；相关请求可通过下方邮箱联系。

本机记录保留至你在 App 中删除、清除 App 数据或卸载；系统备份需通过相应系统管理。服务端 AI 结果通常缓存约 24 小时，用于安全重试；缓存清理不等于清除用量和点数账本。邀请码、审计、用量及支持记录会按提供服务、排障、安全和适用法律所需保留，必要的备份可能在轮换时清除。

如需访问、更正或删除我们持有的数据，请邮件联系维护者，描述请求；我们可能通过私密渠道核实请求与记录的关联。不要发送完整邀请码或密钥作为第一封邮件。我们会告知可处理的范围、保留原因及进展。Mise 无法直接删除 DeepSeek、Apple、GitHub 等第三方独立保留的数据，可另行联系对应服务商。

更新与适用范围

本政策描述当前厨房 App、AI 服务和项目网站。不包含尚未开放的付费内购或账号同步；引入这些功能时会相应更新。Mise 不面向儿童设计。政策有实质变化时会更新本页，并在需要时重新征求同意。

Privacy policy

Updated October 7, 2026

Mise is provided by the project maintainer. Contact zyfeleven@gmail.com for privacy, access, correction or deletion requests. Do not post invite codes or private records in public GitHub issues.

What we process

Inventory, recipes, shopping, meal plans, cooking records and Agent task/action history are primarily stored on your device. Mise does not currently offer accounts or cloud kitchen synchronization. Operating-system backups may separately retain device data according to your settings.

When you use AI, your text, selected images, recipe links and relevant kitchen context, task goals, conversation and tool results are sent to Mise’s Hong Kong server. Content needed for AI is forwarded to DeepSeek for recognition, recipe generation and task decisions. Selecting a photo does not upload your entire photo library.

The server retains invite verification/management records, request identifiers, times, models, token usage, credit entries and technical errors for service operation, safe retries, troubleshooting and abuse prevention. Network access logs may contain connection information such as IP addresses. Information you email us is used to answer your request.

Free AI allowance in public-access versions

In versions offering public free access, an explicit claim creates a random installation identity and uses Apple App Attest to verify the app. The app requests a proof from Apple and sends it to Mise for validation; kitchen content is not included. The server retains hashes of the identity and attestation key to associate the credit ledger, prevent duplicate grants and address abuse. It does not retain raw attestation proofs. The app may temporarily cache a proof in operating-system secure storage for network retries and removes it after successful verification.

Claims also use network addresses for short-term abuse limits. The server converts an address to a keyed hash scoped to a UTC day, removes hashes older than a two-day threshold during hourly cleanup, and expires temporary in-memory request counters. Reverse-proxy access logs are subject to their separate retention rules. While the identity exists, one-time grant and attestation hashes remain with security, usage and credit records. Verified mappings that never complete a claim are removed during regular cleanup after 24 hours. This is not a named account or cross-device balance recovery; Apple verification does not guarantee device uniqueness after reinstall. Manual on-device kitchen tools remain available without claiming.

Local backup files

Supported versions let you explicitly export kitchen records, optionally including recipe photos, conversation text summaries and Agent memory. Files are generated on-device and are unencrypted. They exclude invite codes, API keys and credit balances; Mise does not automatically upload backups to its backend. If you save or share a file through cloud storage, email or another app, that service handles it. Keep files private and manage copies yourself. Import previews the contents and replaces local records only after confirmation. Restored conversations are read-only and memory is off by default. This is manual file transfer, not account or cloud synchronization.

Optional Agent memory

Memory is off by default in versions that support it. When enabled, the Agent can look up relevant local history and send a bounded set of confirmed preferences and relevant history excerpts with later AI requests to the server and model provider described above. Inferred preferences are presented for confirmation. Review sources, edit or forget preferences, or turn memory off to stop future use. Memory is stored on-device with no account sync. Forgetting disables future use; supported undo and action records may retain local copies. Turning memory off or forgetting cannot recall data already sent or erase third-party records.

Voluntary beta feedback

Supported App versions let you report a task experience. Opening or previewing the report uploads nothing. Only after confirmation do we receive your rating, optional note, App version, task ID, step status, timing, credits and limited diagnostic events. Automatically attached data excludes conversation text, photos, ingredient details, long-term memory and full invite codes. Your optional note is sent as written; do not include keys or other secrets. Reports are associated with the server-verified AI identity (invite or public allowance identity) for troubleshooting. They do not call AI or consume AI credits. Reports are stored privately on the Hong Kong server and removed by regular cleanup after 90 days; necessary backup copies may clear on rotation. Contact us below for access or deletion requests.

Providers and locations

The backend is hosted on Alibaba Cloud in Hong Kong. DeepSeek’s privacy policy describes processing and storage in mainland China, potentially outside your region. Read DeepSeek’s privacy policy for its data use and retention. We do not promise zero retention or exclusion from model training by third parties.

Voice input uses the operating system’s speech recognition, with microphone/speech permission. On-device availability depends on the build, platform and system; system services may process audio. Mise’s backend receives the recognized text you submit. The website is available through GitHub Pages and ChatGPT Sites. The Hong Kong server no longer hosts the website or APK downloads. The hosting service processes connection information; on GitHub Pages, GitHub does so under its privacy statement.

Your choices

New versions with the AI permission screen disclose data sharing before the first AI content request. Decline to keep using manual kitchen tools. Withdraw permission in Settings → Privacy & support to stop future AI requests. Withdrawal does not undo previous transfers or erase service records. If an older version lacks this control, update to a supported version or stop using AI. Manage camera, photo and microphone permissions in system settings. The current app has no advertising-tracking SDK, and we do not sell personal data.

Retention and requests

Public-access versions provide “Delete AI identity and service records” in Settings. After confirmation, this removes the identity’s verification identifiers, credit ledger, request/result cache, model-usage records and voluntary feedback from active server storage. Active AI requests must finish first; network or cleanup failures can be retried. Only allocation counts, grant amounts and UTC days unlinked from the former identity remain, so deletion does not replenish the public allocation. The device credential is replaced by a non-identifying deleted flag; this installation does not automatically claim again and unused credits cannot be recovered. Local kitchen records remain and can be managed separately. This action does not immediately erase independent system or server backups, network logs, support emails or third-party records; contact us below about those requests.

Local records remain until you delete them, clear app data or uninstall; manage system backups separately. AI results are normally cached for about 24 hours for retries. Clearing this cache does not erase usage or credit ledgers. Invite, audit, usage and support records are retained as needed for service operation, security, troubleshooting and applicable legal obligations; necessary backups may clear on rotation.

Email us to request access, correction or deletion. We may privately verify your connection to the records and explain what can be removed, any required retention and progress. Do not include full invite codes or keys in your initial email. For data independently held by DeepSeek, Apple, GitHub or other providers, contact them separately.

Scope and changes

This policy covers the current kitchen app, AI service and website. Paid purchases and account synchronization are not yet available; the policy will be updated when introduced. Mise is not designed for children. Material changes will appear here and we will seek renewed consent when needed.
